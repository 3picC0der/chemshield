# report3: lab data for the Third Report Installment (5 Nov 2026)

Everything here was collected on the physical rig on 5 Nov 2026 unless marked simulation.

| File | What it is | Type |
|---|---|---|
| `s7_pump3_flow.csv` | S7 pump flow, pump 3, 3 weighed 10 s runs of distilled water (51, 55, 54 mL) | MEASURED (graduated cylinder, ~288 rpm bench sketch, one pump) |

Mean 320 mL/min, sample SD 12.5 mL/min (about 1.1 mL per revolution). At the full 400 rpm the same ratio extrapolates to about 460 mL/min; this is an extrapolation, not a measurement.

Not yet in this folder: the pH-recovery trials (2 L half-scale tank, 0.5 M HCl on pump 1), pump 1 calibration weighings and the pH calibration. They are added when those runs are finished. Rehearsals of the 2 L plan were simulation only and are not lab evidence.
